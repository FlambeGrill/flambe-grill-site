Engineering & Taste

Four Pillars of Flambé Grill

Designed for hosts who demand authentic culinary provenance without lifting a finger.

Pillar 01 • Fuel Purity

100% Natural Charcoal Beds Exclusively

We never compromise with propane gas or overpowering wood-fire logs. Our custom stainless-steel pits burn pure natural charcoal exclusively—delivering clean, high-heat infrared radiation that sears proteins while preserving delicate Greek herbs, lemon, and olive oil marinades.

Zero Gas Burners Zero Wood Logs Pure Lump Charcoal
Pillar 02 • Authentic Mechanics

Horizontal Rotisserie Spits

Unlike commercial vertical cones that dry out meat, our heavy-duty horizontal spits turn slowly above glowing charcoal so natural juices continuously baste every layer of gyro and kontosouvli.

Traditional Greek Spit Engineering
Pillar 03 • Grand Centerpiece

Whole Pig & Spring Lamb Roasts

The ultimate visual spectacle for milestone celebrations. Whole heritage pig or tender spring lamb slow-roasted over natural charcoal until the skin shatters like glass.

Carved Live Before Your Guests
Pillar 04 • Complete Gastronomy

Artisanal Greek Classics & Prime Steaks

Beyond the spit, we craft a complete Mediterranean table: hand-skewered souvlaki, prime tomahawk steaks, traditional moussaka, pastitsio, flaky spanakopita, gemista, lemon-oregano roasted potatoes, and thick house-made tzatziki.
